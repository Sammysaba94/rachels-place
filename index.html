The Program

Introducing עלית [a-leet]

את עלית על כולנה (משלי לא, כט)

You are elevated above them all

Learning, at the pace of real life

Rooted in Torah, Alit is a learning program built for those who are reaching higher, who care about their growth and want to build a special future. There’s going through life, and then there’s owning that life. Join and see the difference.

Rooted in Our Shul and Rabbi's Hashkafa

Every class is shaped by shared Mesorah, taught with clarity and conveyed with no judgement
